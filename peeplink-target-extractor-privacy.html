Privacy Policy for Peeplink Target Extractor

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Peeplink Target Extractor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user activity, or browsing history with external servers or third parties.

Local processing

All operations, including URL scanning, target extraction, and history management, are executed entirely locally on the user's device.

The extension uses the browser's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) only to support its local functionality. No data ever leaves the user's browser.

Data sharing

Peeplink Target Extractor does not sell, transfer, disclose, or share data with any external service, advertiser, analytics provider, or other third party.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.
